# Minerva — Data Moat, Year-Two Pitch (Template — Do Not Use Live Yet)

This is the sharper, numbers-filled version of Rule 5 in
`minerva_competitive_strategy.docx` ("the data advantage compounds over
time"). That rule is currently true in principle but has nothing real
behind it yet — no client has 12 months of `agent_insights` data. **Do not
use anything in this file on a real sales call until the bracketed numbers
below are filled in with real figures from real client data.** Using this
pitch before then would be exactly the kind of AI-adjacent overclaim
`SALES_CLAIMS_ACCURACY_NOTE.md` exists to prevent.

## When this becomes usable

Earliest: 3 months after the first cohort of clients has been live with the
Agent Operating System running (not just signed up — actually generating
`agent_insights` rows weekly). Realistic target: 6-12 months in, once
there's a large enough client base that suburb/job-type patterns are
statistically meaningful rather than one business's noise.

## The pitch shape (fill in brackets with real numbers when ready)

> "Here's something no competitor can sell you on day one, because it
> doesn't exist on day one for anyone — including us, when we started.
> Minerva clients who've been live for [X months] have generated [Y] rows
> of job, location, pricing, and lead data. That data tells us things like:
> [which suburbs converted quotes to bookings at the highest rate], [which
> job types were most likely to reactivate from a cold lead after 90+ days],
> [which price points held without losing the job]. A brand-new ServiceTitan
> or Procore implementation gives you zero of that on day one. Minerva at
> month one is a GPS and scheduling tool. Minerva at month twelve knows your
> market better than you do, because it's been watching every job, every
> price, every suburb the whole time. That's not a feature you can buy —
> it's a reason to have started earlier rather than later."

## What has to be true before this is said out loud to a prospect

- [ ] At least one real, specific, non-fabricated insight exists that a
      client's `agent_insights` rows actually produced (e.g., "Client X's
      data showed suburb A converts 40% more reliably than suburb B for job
      type Y") — with the real client's permission to reference it, even
      anonymized.
- [ ] The number of months/rows referenced is the true current figure, not
      a rounded-up estimate — update this file's placeholder numbers the
      day you have real ones, same rule as everywhere else in this project.
- [ ] This has been checked against `SALES_CLAIMS_ACCURACY_NOTE.md`'s "no
      learning/adapting" rule — the pitch above is carefully worded around
      "watching and accumulating data," NOT "learning" or "training" in the
      ML sense. Keep it that way when filling in real numbers; don't let a
      real success story tempt a rewrite into ML-sounding language that
      still isn't true.

## Industrial (Client 9) version — even further out

The Trade-sector version above at least has a plausible 3-6 month runway.
The industrial sector has zero clients yet as of this writing — this
version doesn't get filled in until Client 9 has been sold at all, let
alone live long enough to generate a real pattern. Placeholder shape only:

> "Minerva Industrial clients build a real record of site history — check-
> ins, safety incidents, consumables usage, engine-hours trends — that
> becomes the underlying evidence for faster client verification packages
> and, eventually, predictive maintenance flags before a breakdown rather
> than after one. That second part isn't built yet. Don't promise it."

Do not use even the Trade version of this pitch until the checklist above
is genuinely satisfied — an unfilled version of this pitch is worse than no
pitch at all, because "we have a data advantage" said with nothing behind
it is precisely the kind of claim that erodes the founder-credibility angle
Minerva's whole positioning depends on.
