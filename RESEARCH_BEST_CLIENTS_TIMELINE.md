# Research: highest-acceptance-likelihood clients, ranked, with dated timelines

## Methodology (so the ranking isn't a guess)

Searched public review platforms (ProductReview.com.au, Trustpilot) across
six Australian trade/service verticals not yet fully mined in prior
research rounds — plumbing, electrical, HVAC, commercial cleaning, pest
control, garage doors, solar installation — specifically looking for a
recurring, verifiable, *operational* complaint pattern (no-shows, missed
ETAs, no communication, scattered site verification) that Minerva's real,
already-built features actually solve: live GPS tracking, automatic ETA
texts, GPS-timestamped job records, multi-site arrival logging.

**Two new real candidates surfaced** this round beyond the five already in
`OUTREACH_DRAFTS_TOP_PICKS.md`. Two more verticals (garage doors, solar)
were searched and explicitly rejected — reasoning below, because "rejected
with a reason" is as useful as "accepted."

**Rejected verticals, and why:** garage door repair and solar installation
complaints skew toward deceptive pricing, non-compliant/unsafe
workmanship, and dishonesty about work actually performed (e.g. an
Electrical Regulator finding non-compliant solar wiring 18 months after
sale) — not a logistics/communication gap. A business whose core problem
is the honesty of the work itself has no real incentive to adopt a tool
that makes its operations more visible and auditable, and pitching one
risks associating Minerva with a company under regulatory scrutiny. Same
reasoning already applied to Metropolitan Plumbing's "predatory pricing"
caveat in prior research — applied consistently here, not selectively.

**Every dollar figure below is an estimate range pending a real discovery
call — none of these technician counts are confirmed.** Consistent with
every prior draft's rule: ask real fleet size and pricing tier fit on the
actual call, don't quote a number before that.

## Target 7 — Premium Air (NEW — severe pain, high caveat, read before sending anything)

**The pain signal is the strongest found in this entire research round:**
public reviews describe 13 occasions of failing to respond at all, 17
occasions of customers being directly lied to about appointment status,
3 cancelled appointments, a $15,000 installation left unfinished, and
multiple formal complaints lodged with the ACCC and Consumer Affairs
Victoria. The company has 20+ years in business and 100,000+ installs
claimed on its own site — real scale, not a fly-by-night operation.

**Why this ranks near the bottom of the list despite the strongest pain
signal, stated plainly:** a complaint pattern this severe (customers
calling the company "dishonest, incapable and incompetent," an unfinished
$15,000 job, active regulator complaints) suggests the core problem may
not be "no GPS tracking software" — it may be understaffing, overselling
beyond real capacity, or a deeper operational/honesty problem that no
software layer fixes. Two real risks specific to this target:
1. **Software can't fix a trust problem that's already this severe.**
   Minerva's live tracking and automatic ETA texts solve "we didn't tell
   the customer" — they don't solve "we told the customer something false
   on purpose" or "we don't have the capacity to do the job we sold."
   Pitching this account risks a wasted sales cycle if the real issue is
   the latter, not the former.
2. **Reputational association risk to Minerva.** A company under active
   ACCC/Consumer Affairs complaint is a name you may not want attached to
   a "client" list at all, regardless of deal size — this is worth
   weighing independent of the revenue math.

**Recommendation:** include this as an opportunistic, low-priority send
only — not a plan centerpiece — and go in eyes-open on both risks above.
If pursued, the honest discovery question is different from every other
draft on this list: ask directly what's driving the response-time and
communication gap (is it a tooling gap or a capacity/staffing gap) before
proposing anything, since the answer determines whether Minerva is even
the right fix.
