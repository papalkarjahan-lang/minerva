# Minerva acquisition ad campaigns — drafted copy, nothing spent yet

**Nothing here has been launched, and no ad account has been created or
funded.** This is copy/targeting for your review only. `launch-ad-campaign`
in the codebase is a different thing — it's a per-customer feature letting
a Minerva *client* run their own ads from their own Meta account; it is
not usable for Minerva's own acquisition marketing. Actually running any
of this requires you personally creating (or already having) a Google Ads
and/or Meta Business account, funding it with your own payment method, and
giving the final go-ahead on spend — none of which I can or will do
autonomously.

**Before spending anything, decide two things and tell me:**
1. **Budget** — a monthly number you're comfortable testing with. Real
   benchmark for local-service SaaS cost-per-acquisition: **$30-60 per
   trial signup**. At $500/mo that's roughly 8-16 signups/month; at
   $1,500/mo roughly 25-50/month — directly moves the Track C self-serve
   numbers in `GRAND_PLAN_END_OF_2026.md` §4.
2. **Platform** — Google Search (intent-based — someone already searching
   "plumbing dispatch software") usually converts higher but costs more
   per click; Meta (interest/demographic-based — small business owners in
   trade Facebook groups, trade-page audiences) is cheaper per click but
   lower intent. Running both eventually is reasonable; starting with one
   is the honest way to learn your real numbers first.

## Google Search Ads — draft copy (intent-based)

**Campaign structure:** one campaign, ad groups split by trade (Plumbing /
Electrical / HVAC / Pest Control / Locksmith), since each has a slightly
different pain point per `EXACT_OUTREACH_EMAIL_DRAFTS.md`.

**Keywords (exact/phrase match, negative-match "free" and "jobs" to avoid
job-seeker traffic):**
- "plumbing dispatch software", "electrician job scheduling app", "hvac
  technician tracking", "gps tracking for tradesmen", "field service
  management software australia", "job dispatch software small business"

**Ad copy (3 headline variants to A/B test, per ad group — Plumbing shown,
same structure for other trades swapping the trade word):**

> **Headline 1:** GPS Tracking for Plumbing Vans | Minerva
> **Headline 2:** Stop "Where's My Plumber" Calls
> **Headline 3:** 7-Day Free Trial, No Card Required
> **Description:** Live van tracking, automatic client ETA texts, no app
> install. $49-119/tech/month. Set up in 20 minutes. Try free for 7 days.

**Landing page:** send to `/pricing` (not the homepage) — a click on
"plumbing dispatch software" is already sold on the category, `/pricing`
answers "how much" immediately instead of re-selling the concept.
